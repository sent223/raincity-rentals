# Design Research

## Sites reviewed
| Site | Type | What works | What doesn't|
|------|------|------------|-------------|
| Turo| P2P| Clear car photos,prices & car display per city| Too many checkout steps|
| Enterprise | National | Good homepage design, Great filtering car system | long checkout steps, mobile app abit sluggish|
| SIXT | National | Transaarent pricing, clear | Limitedcar options, lengthy checkout steps |
| Luxcar | Local | Premium dark layout, clear airport delivery messaging | Lacks detailed fleet search filters, clear on-line bookings only |

## Patterns I will use
-Search bar above the fold on the home page
-Clear search filters for cars
-Shorter checkout steps
-Clear car details
-clear car pricing 

## Patterns I will avoid
-hidden pricing till the last steps
-Very many checkout steps
-Dull colors and home page design.

## Colors and imagery noticed
-Common colors: white, light blue, light green.
-Photo style: real-world & 3D render/ vector isolated car graphic

## Raincity Rentals design direction
- **Palette:** evergreen #1F4D3A, Puget Sound blue #2F6F8F, slate grey
  background, warm yellow accent (buttons)
- **Tone:** friendly, local, practical ("rain or shine")
- **Seattle-specific features:** EV/AWD/roof-rack filters, Sea-Tac pickup
  highlight, rainy-day deals banner
